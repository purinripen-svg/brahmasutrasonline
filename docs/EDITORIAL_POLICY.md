# BrahmaSutrasOnline Editorial Policy

Version: 1.0

## Mission

BrahmaSutrasOnline aims to provide a free, accurate, modern, and academically responsible digital edition of the Brahma Sūtras.

---

## Editorial Principles

1. Preserve the Sanskrit text faithfully according to the selected source edition.
2. Use IAST transliteration consistently.
3. Clearly distinguish:
   - Original Sanskrit text
   - English translation
   - Editorial explanation
   - Traditional commentary
4. Attribute all external sources.
5. Never modify the Sanskrit text without documenting the source.

---

## Translation Policy

The English translation published on BrahmaSutrasOnline will be:

- Original where possible, or
- Used under an appropriate license with attribution.

Translations should aim to be:

- Accurate
- Literal where appropriate
- Readable modern English
- Neutral with respect to doctrinal interpretation

---

## Commentary Policy

Traditional commentaries are presented as independent viewpoints.

Examples include:

- Śaṅkara (Advaita)
- Rāmānuja (Viśiṣṭādvaita)
- Madhva (Dvaita)

Editorial summaries should never be presented as quotations.

---

## Data Standards

Every sūtra record includes:

- ID
- Adhyāya
- Pāda
- Sūtra number
- Sanskrit
- IAST
- Translation
- Word meanings
- References
- Commentary metadata

---

## Version Control

All editorial changes should be tracked through Git.

Major content revisions should be documented in CHANGELOG.md.

---

## Long-Term Goal

To become a trusted and freely accessible reference for the study of the Brahma Sūtras.
